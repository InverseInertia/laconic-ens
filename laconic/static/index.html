LENS

Math keys

These keys edit the source text. The result column shows the typeset math. They are inactive on comment lines (starting with #) and can be switched off with the Math keys box.

/Fraction. Takes the term on the left as numerator and the term on the right, if any, as denominator. a/ gives a/( ). After A. it gives A./
\Radical. sqrt( ), or wraps the selection
.Subscript after a name. M.max gives M_max. After a number it stays a decimal point
^Exponent slot. x^( )
→ or )Leave a slot. If it holds one simple term the parentheses go away: x^(2) becomes x^2
EnterWith only closing brackets left on the line, finishes the line instead of splitting it
BackspaceIn an empty slot, removes the slot and its ^, / or sqrt. After , or ; , removes the separator and its space together
Ctrl+MArray. [ ], or wraps the selection
, ;A space is added after them automatically. In an array, , separates columns and ; separates rows
Ctrl+T
Alt+T		Transpose the term on the left or the selection. Press again to undo. Browsers reserve Ctrl+T for a new tab, so use Alt+T in a normal tab
Ctrl+GGreek letter for the character before the caret. Press again to go back. Edit the mapping under Greek
Ctrl+/
Ctrl+\		Type a literal / or \ with no fraction or radical slot
= + - *Spaces are added around them (and around ->, .*, ./ and fractions), and a second space is ignored. A sign (-3, x^-1) and 1e-3 stay tight. Backspace removes a spaced operator in one press
~A name that starts with ~ is global and survives !clear
!clearOn its own line, removes every non-global variable above it
'Starts a unit: 12'kN/'m. Bare names are always variables
